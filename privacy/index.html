Privacy

Mitsumon keeps practice on your device unless you choose cloud features or explicitly send something to the optional AI study partner.

On this device

Trainer profiles, progress, game data and workbook writing are saved in your browser. Torah practice recordings stay in the running page’s memory: that feature does not upload them or include them in cloud saves or backups. Closing or reloading the page clears those recordings.

The no-sign-in study guide uses prepared hints from the current lesson. It does not contact an AI provider or send anything you type or record. The optional live AI study partner is limited to adult testers the developer explicitly approves. Child/family AI access, including supervised children’s voice and writing, is not enabled. Parental supervision or an adult-owned account alone does not turn it on.

In approved adult AI practice, Ask or Review sends your message, recent conversation and any notes you choose to include through Supabase to OpenAI. Recording requires a separate microphone action; it stays in page memory until you explicitly send it for transcription. Voice turns are limited to 45 seconds. OpenAI can generate a spoken reply when you select that option. AI chat and audio are not saved to your local profile or cloud save; closing the AI panel clears its conversation. Supabase stores usage counters and successful review identifiers, learning-goal identifiers and timestamps, not the messages, notes, recordings or transcripts. AI can make mistakes; its reviews do not certify chanting or pronunciation.

Olam Ivrit’s Hebrew machine keeps its typed Hebrew in the open panel’s memory. It resolves known words and fixed sound units on this device; it does not send your writing for speech generation. Lesson clips are prepared by the developer using fixed text and served from Supabase. Hebrew sound-matching and prayer-order quests save completion goals in the existing local learning progress. The B’mitzvah date finder calculates on this device; the entered date stays in the page’s memory. Applying its suggestion saves the portion and practice reference, not the ceremony date.

Optional sign-in

Mitsumon is in private testing. Accounts are by invitation only and open to adults only for now. Cloud features work only for accounts the developer activates.

Email/password and Google sign-in use Supabase Auth. Google sign-in shares basic account information, such as your email and profile details, with Supabase. Sign-in checks your existing cloud save and wallet; it does not upload or overwrite local work automatically. Save to cloud uploads the selected companion, progress and workbook writing. Online battles store participant identifiers, companion and game state, outcomes, timestamps and prizes; opponents can see their match data.

Sign-in sessions stay in page memory. Google sign-in temporarily uses this tab’s storage to complete its redirect. Connecting AI practice from a game panel uses a temporary connection code in the account tab and passes a short-lived sign-in token to the same-origin study window after you press Connect. That token stays in page memory.

Disconnecting and deleting

Sign out ends the current sign-in but leaves local profiles and writing. Clear this site’s browser data on each device to remove those copies, and delete downloaded backups separately.

There is no self-service account deletion. Contact benyaminnewman@gmail.com to request review or deletion of account and cloud data. No automatic deletion schedule is configured for cloud saves, battles or AI review receipts. AI usage counters older than 31 days are removed when new AI requests reserve usage; deleting an account removes its AI counters and receipts. Battle invitations expire after 24 hours, but that does not delete their records. Service-provider logs and backups have their own retention policies; their retention periods have not been verified.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, cloud storage and server functions. Authorized project administrators can access server-held data. Google handles Google sign-in. GitHub Pages hosts the site. PocketTorah audio streams from GitHub, World ORT audio from ScrollScraper, and prayer recordings from archive.org.

The optional Torah-story and history TV loads BimBam videos from YouTube only when you choose to load a video. Entering the room or browsing its channel guide does not contact YouTube or load its thumbnails. Loading a video shares your IP address, browser information and this site’s origin with YouTube/Google. The player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed domain, but YouTube’s policies still apply; it may show ads, recommendations or outside links. Changing channels, turning off the TV or leaving removes the player. We do not record video watch history or award learning points for watching.

These requests disclose your IP address, ordinary browser/connection information and the requested page or recording to the provider. Optional source links open outside sites under their own policies. The reviewed app code has no advertising or behavioral-analytics SDK. OpenAI processes explicitly submitted adult AI text and voice requests. Turning off response storage in our requests does not establish zero data retention at OpenAI; its account-specific retention rules still apply. ElevenLabs generates optional, explicitly requested adult voice previews from fixed Hebrew lesson text or quest lines selected by the server. We do not send learner recordings, writing or names to ElevenLabs. Hebrew voice previews are experimental, not verified pronunciation references, and award no points. These previews travel through Supabase; the browser does not connect directly to ElevenLabs. Provider retention settings have not been independently verified.
